About NeuroScan AI

Advancing neurological research with explainable artificial intelligence

🎯 Our Mission

Parkinson's disease-related dementia affects millions worldwide, yet early detection remains a significant clinical challenge. NeuroScan AI leverages state-of-the-art deep learning to assist medical researchers and clinicians in identifying dementia stages from brain MRI scans with high accuracy and full explainability.

This project was developed as a Final Year Project focused on applying AI to healthcare, combining rigorous machine learning research with a user-friendly clinical interface.

🤖 The AI Model

🏗️

Architecture

EfficientNet-B3 — A highly efficient convolutional neural network designed using neural architecture search. Fine-tuned on the Parkinson's Disease dementia dataset with custom classification head.

📊

Dataset

Trained on a comprehensive dataset of brain MRI scans spanning 5 dementia stages with data augmentation techniques including rotation, flipping, color jitter, and random cropping to improve generalization.

🔬

GradCAM Explainability

Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping generates visual heatmaps showing which brain regions most influenced the model's classification decision — critical for clinical trust.

⚡

Performance

Achieved 94.6% validation accuracy after 100+ training epochs with cosine annealing learning rate scheduling and early stopping to prevent overfitting.
